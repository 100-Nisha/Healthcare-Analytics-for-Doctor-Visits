# 🏥 Healthcare Analytics for Doctor Visits

## 📌 Project Overview

Healthcare organizations collect large amounts of patient-related data every day. Understanding how factors such as age, gender, income, illness severity, and health insurance affect doctor visits can help healthcare providers improve patient care and resource planning.

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a healthcare dataset to identify patterns in doctor visits, healthcare utilization, income distribution, insurance coverage, and illness impact. Using Python and data visualization techniques, the project transforms raw healthcare data into meaningful insights for healthcare decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Healthcare providers need to understand the factors that influence patient visits and healthcare utilization. However, large healthcare datasets often contain hidden patterns that are difficult to identify without proper analysis.

The objective of this project is to analyze healthcare data and discover how demographic, economic, and health-related factors impact doctor visits and healthcare service usage.

## 📊 Visualizations Included

### 1. Income Distribution (Box Plot)

Analyzes minimum, maximum, median income, and outliers.

### 2. Missing Values Heatmap

Identifies missing values and data quality issues.

### 3. Correlation Heatmap

Shows relationships between healthcare variables.

### 4. Income vs Doctor Visits (Scatter Plot)

Examines the relationship between patient income and hospital visits.

### 5. Gender-Based Illness Analysis

Compares illness distribution among males and females.

### 6. Government Insurance (Low Income)

Shows percentage of patients receiving government health insurance due to low income.

### 7. Private Health Insurance Analysis

Analyzes private health insurance coverage among patients.

### 8. Government Insurance (Old Age/Disability)

Shows healthcare coverage for elderly and disabled individuals.

Compares reduced activity days caused by illness across genders.
